### Reviewer checklist (required separately for every proposed objective)

A qualified school teacher with relevant Class 1 English and medium expertise must provide documented, attributable review through an approved school process. Review must cover:

1. Official book identity, 2025–26 edition, exact chapter, source and any copyright/permission restrictions.
2. Educational correctness and developmental suitability for a child who may not be able to read yet.
3. Accurate Hindi and Marathi wording reviewed independently; **support language is not school-medium authorization**.
4. Speech/visual teaching sequence including gestures, pictures, gentle correction, alternate explanations, privacy-safe roleplay, and short spoken homework.
5. Independent check not contaminated by teaching hints; delayed recall separate from a first correct response; no grade or mastery awarded from silence, STT errors, repetition or an AI lexical match.
6. Rights for any word bank, poem line, QR code, asset, recorded voice or artwork, and truthful audio availability.
7. Chronological prerequisite/order chart: exactly when each objective is first taught, revisited, practiced and checked in a concrete **10-level** schedule, including sources from later units. Preserve existing English Balbharati `g01-l01` student progress.
8. School admin explicitly authorizes the book track and grade. Student support language and student PIN never choose the book.
9. A reviewed, immutable curriculum content revision and mapping to Node-owned canonical lesson and activity IDs.

**No teacher review signature is generated by this repository.** A human approval event should reference a reviewer identity/role, objective ID, content digest/revision, textbook edition, decision date and approval scope. Do not silently set `reviewStatus` to approved from CI, a JSON edit or an AI response; independently verify all approvals against school authorization before runtime publication.
